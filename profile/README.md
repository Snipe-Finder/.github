## Snipe Finder

Free eBay listing alerts. Make an alert with keywords and filters for price, condition, seller feedback and more, and Snipe Finder checks it every few minutes. Each new match is sent to you once: as a browser notification, by email, or in a private Discord thread.

- **Website:** [snipefinder.com](https://snipefinder.com/?ref=github)
- **Guides:** [getting notified of new listings, keeping junk out, and more](https://snipefinder.com/guides?ref=github)
- **Discord server:** [discord.gg/5wjc3yQhEp](https://discord.gg/5wjc3yQhEp)

### Free to use

- [**ebay-search-exclusion-words**](https://github.com/Snipe-Finder/ebay-search-exclusion-words): hand-picked words that leave fakes, empty boxes, parts and accessories out of eBay searches, for 12 kinds of item. Reuse them anywhere under CC BY 4.0.

The code for the app itself isn't public.
